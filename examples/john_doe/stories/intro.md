# Tell me about yourself (the canonical intro)

How a prep uses this: copy it, keep its order and its figures, and change only what the round calls for.
A procurement-heavy role can put the Coupa paragraph first. The closing line is always written fresh for
the role, from the posting or from what the interviewer said the team wants. 60 to 90 seconds. Stop after
the closing line.

## Short
I'm the product owner for finance systems at Northwind Traders, a distributor, and I've been there 4.5 years. I own our NetSuite ERP across order-to-cash and procure-to-pay, and I chair the weekly intake meeting for the finance systems team.

## Full (STARR)
I'm the product owner for finance systems at Northwind Traders, a distributor, and I've been there 4.5 years. I own our NetSuite ERP across order-to-cash and procure-to-pay, and I chair the weekly intake meeting where the finance systems team decides what gets built next.

Two pieces of work I'd point to. I led the Coupa procurement rollout to 3 business units and 420 requesters, one business unit at a time, with requester training before each go-live. And I ran the month-end close program, which took the close from 9 working days to 6.

Day to day, I prioritize the backlog, I coordinate user acceptance testing and release sign-off for our quarterly ERP releases, and I build the SQL and Excel reporting finance leadership uses.
