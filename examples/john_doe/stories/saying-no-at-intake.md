# Saying no at intake

## Short
A region asked at our intake meeting for a custom Coupa approval workflow. I turned it down, showed them the standard approval flow already covered their case, and they adopted the standard flow.

## Full (STARR)
I chair the weekly intake meeting for the finance systems team, and every request is ranked on value, effort and risk.

One region asked for a custom Coupa approval workflow. My task was to answer them without building something only one region would use.

I turned it down, but not with a bare no. I walked through their case against the standard approval flow and showed them it was already covered. The ranked list is shared with requesters, so they could see where a custom build would have landed and why.

The region adopted the standard flow.

What I learned: a no lands when the evidence is in the same meeting. Showing them their own case in the standard flow did more than any argument about maintenance would have.
