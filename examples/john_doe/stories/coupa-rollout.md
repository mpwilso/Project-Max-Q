# Rolling Coupa out to 420 requesters

## Short
I led our Coupa procurement rollout to 3 business units and 420 requesters. I took it one business unit at a time, ran requester training before each go-live, and owned the cutover checklist every unit went through.

## Full (STARR)
Northwind was moving procurement into Coupa, across 3 business units and 420 requesters. I was already the product owner for our NetSuite ERP, procure-to-pay included, so the rollout came to me.

My job was to get every one of those requesters buying through Coupa.

I chose to go one business unit at a time rather than all at once. Before each go-live I ran requester training, and I owned a cutover checklist that every unit went through step by step, so a go-live never depended on someone remembering what the last one did.

All 3 business units and 420 requesters moved onto Coupa.

What I'd keep: one unit at a time. It cost calendar time, and it meant each go-live ran on a checklist that had already been through a real one.
