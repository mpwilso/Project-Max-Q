# Taking the month-end close from 9 days to 6

## Short
I ran our month-end close program. The close went from 9 working days to 6, and by my estimate it freed 120 analyst hours a month.

## Full (STARR)
Our month-end close took 9 working days, and I owned the program to shorten it.

I started by mapping every close task with the accounting team, so I could see where the days went before I changed anything.

Then I moved the bank reconciliations and the accrual entries into NetSuite saved searches, and I replaced the end-of-month status email with a daily close stand-up.

The close went from 9 working days to 6. My estimate from the accounting team's timesheets is that it freed 120 analyst hours a month.

What I'd do the same way: map the work before changing any of it. The saved searches helped because the map showed which tasks were eating the days.
