# A go-live I had to roll back

## Short
The Coupa cutover for our third business unit was rolled back after 2 days. Its supplier records had never been cleaned, and my checklist had no step for that. I ran the cleanup of 1,800 duplicate supplier records, added the check, and the unit went live 5 weeks later.

## Full (STARR)
I was rolling Coupa out one business unit at a time on a cutover checklist I owned, and the first units had gone live on it.

The third unit's cutover was rolled back after 2 days. Its supplier records had never been cleaned, and that was my miss: the checklist had no step for supplier data.

I owned the fix. I ran the cleanup of 1,800 duplicate supplier records, and I added a supplier-data check to the checklist so no unit could reach a cutover date without it.

The unit went live 5 weeks later.

What I learned: a checklist only covers what the last go-live taught it. Now I ask what the next unit has that the earlier ones didn't, before I set a date.
