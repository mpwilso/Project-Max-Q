# Fit rubric (example)

Score how well one job posting fits the candidate profile, out of 100, from the posting's daily work
rather than its wish list.

| Area | Points | What earns them |
|---|---|---|
| Role match | 30 | The job is product, program or product-owner work in the candidate's lane |
| Skills and tools | 25 | The required skills and systems are ones the profile shows |
| Seniority | 20 | The required years and level are within reach of the profile |
| Domain | 15 | The business area (finance systems, ERP, procurement, operations) matches |
| Logistics | 10 | Location, remote policy and work authorization work for the candidate |

Rules:
1. Charge a gap once, in one area. A missing preferred qualification costs 1 to 2 points.
2. A hard blocker (a people-manager core, a required certification, a location the candidate cannot
   work in) belongs in `dealbreakers`, and the score should reflect it.
3. Use only facts in the profile. Do not assume experience the profile does not state.

Verdicts: 75 and above is `apply`, 55 to 74 is `maybe`, below 55 is `skip`.
